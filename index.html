The studio

zyro is a small, independent studio. We make the games we want to play: tight controls, honest mechanics and visuals that stand out, with no pay-to-win.

  1. 01

    Real 3D

    Every game is rendered in full 3D with custom shaders and lighting.

  2. 02

    Touch first

    Native iOS and Android builds, designed from the ground up for touch.

  3. 03

    Fair by design

    Progression you earn and difficulty you can master. Never bought.

  4. 04

    Gameplay first

    No ads-first design. Monetisation never dictates the level design.

Process

How a zyro game gets made

  1. 01

    Prototype

    One core mechanic, built fast and judged on a single question: is it fun in the first ten seconds?

  2. 02

    Playtest

    Real players on real devices. We watch, measure and cut anything that adds friction.

  3. 03

    Polish

    Lighting, shaders, haptics and sound, then level tuning until the difficulty curve feels earned.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    Launch on the App Store and Google Play, then keep improving based on player feedback.

FAQ

Questions

Anything else? Write to hello@zyro.games.

When will the games be available?

Our titles are in development. Store links will appear on each game's page as soon as it launches. Email us if you'd like to hear about releases.

Which devices are supported?

Every zyro game is built natively for iOS and Android, and designed for touch screens first.

Are your games pay-to-win?

No. Progression is earned through play, and difficulty is something you master, not something you buy your way past.

How do I report a bug?

Email hello@zyro.games with the game name, your device model and OS version, and what happened. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.

Can I write about your games?

Yes, please. Email us with your outlet and we'll send icons, screenshots and game information.
